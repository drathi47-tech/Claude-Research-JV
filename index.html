Methodology

How companies are selected, scored, and classified in the Consumer Trend Radar

⚡

Company Selection Criteria

REQUIRED
30% YoY Growth Threshold

Companies must show at least 30% year-over-year growth across composite signals (Google Trends + E-commerce + Traffic + Social) to be included in the tracker.

EXCEPTION
Social Breakout Override

Brands below 30% composite growth can be included if they demonstrate breakout social signals — viral content, community-driven advocacy, or exceptional sentiment momentum that indicate imminent growth inflection.

EXCLUDED
Acquired Brands

Companies that have been acquired are removed from tracking (e.g., Neemli Naturals → GOAT Brand Labs, Earth Rhythm → Nykaa). Acquisition removes them from the emerging D2C opportunity set.

EXCLUDED
Pre-Traction Brands

Pre-seed companies with no measurable market traction are excluded. Signals require sufficient data points for meaningful scoring (minimum 6 months of trackable activity).

EXCLUDED
Listed Conglomerate Subsidiaries

Brands owned by publicly listed groups are excluded as they are not VC-investable standalone D2C opportunities (e.g., Bewakoof → Aditya Birla Group).

REQUIRED
Revenue Floor: INR 40Cr/yr Minimum

Brands must demonstrate at least INR 40Cr/yr revenue to be tracked. Shark Tank visibility or social buzz alone does not qualify without proportionate commercial traction.

Signal Scoring Formulas

G

Google Trends

25% weight
(90d momentum x 0.5) + (current / peak_12m x 50)
  • 90-day momentum (50%) — Percentage change in Google search interest over the past 90 days, measuring directional trend
  • Peak proximity (50%) — How close the current search index is to the brand's 12-month peak, measuring sustained interest
  • Data source — Weekly search interest index from Google Trends (52-week history)
  • Range — 0 to 100 (capped)
★

E-commerce Reviews

20% weight
(amazon_sentiment + myntra_sentiment) / 2
  • Amazon sentiment — Positive-to-negative review ratio weighted by recency on Amazon India
  • Myntra sentiment — Same analysis for Myntra reviews (higher weight for fashion/beauty brands)
  • Data points — Total reviews, avg rating, rating change, review velocity, sentiment score
  • Range — 0 to 100
↗

Website Traffic

30% weight (highest)
min(100, MoM_growth% x 2 + 50)
  • Month-over-month growth — Percentage change in estimated monthly visits vs previous month
  • Baseline 50 — A score of 50 = flat traffic; above 50 = growing; below 50 = declining
  • Why highest weight — Website traffic is the strongest leading indicator of commercial intent and conversion potential
  • Range — 0 to 100 (capped)
☼

Social Sentiment

25% weight
(reddit + instagram + linkedin sentiment) / 3
  • Reddit — Upvote ratios, comment tone analysis, and community engagement in relevant subreddits
  • Instagram — Comment sentiment, engagement rate, and share velocity from brand mentions
  • LinkedIn — Professional sentiment from posts, comments, and founder thought leadership
  • Range — 0 to 100

Composite Score Formula
